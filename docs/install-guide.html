Jr. Lancers Basketball App

Installation Guide

Welcome to the Jr. Lancers! This guide will help you install the team app on your phone so you can access schedules, stats, chat, and more.

What is the Jr. Lancers App?

The Jr. Lancers app is a Progressive Web App (PWA) - a website that works like a native app on your phone. Once installed, it appears on your home screen just like any other app.

📅 Game schedules & locations
📊 Live game stats
🏀 Animated playbook
💬 Team chat & announcements
👥 Player roster & contacts
📸 Game highlights
✋ Attendance & volunteering
🔔 Push notifications

Step 1: Open the App in Your Browser

  1. Open your phone's browser:
    • iPhone/iPad: Use Safari (required)
    • Android: Use Chrome (recommended)
  2. Go to: lancers-bball.web.app
  3. You'll see the login screen

Login screen

iPhone users: You MUST use Safari. The "Add to Home Screen" option is not available in Chrome or other browsers on iOS.

Step 2: Login or Register

Important: You must login FIRST before installing the app to your home screen.

  1. If you have an account: Enter your email and password, tap "Sign In"
  2. If you're new: Tap "Register", enter your email (must be pre-approved by Coach), create a password
  3. Verify your email if prompted
  4. Once logged in, you'll see the home screen with your player info

Home screen after logging in

Step 3: Install to Home Screen

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Tap the menu icon

2. Tap "Share"

3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"

4. Tap "Add"

  1. Tap the menu icon (three lines) at the bottom of Safari
  2. Tap "Share" in the menu that appears
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right corner
  5. Done! The Jr. Lancers icon appears on your home screen
Can't find "Add to Home Screen"?
  • Make sure you're using Safari (not Chrome)
  • Scroll down in the share menu - it's not always visible at first
  • If using iOS 17+, you may need to scroll horizontally to find it

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top right corner of Chrome
  2. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
  3. Tap "Install" to confirm
  4. Done! The Jr. Lancers icon appears on your home screen
Android alternative: Some phones show an "Install" banner at the bottom of the screen automatically. Tap it to install.

Step 4: Enable Notifications

After logging in, you'll be prompted to enable push notifications. We recommend enabling them to stay updated on:

  • New chat messages
  • Coach announcements
  • RSVP and volunteer reminders
  • Game starting alerts
  1. Tap "Enable" when prompted in the app
  2. Tap "Allow" when your phone asks for permission
  3. Done! You'll receive notifications for team updates
Missed the prompt? You can enable notifications later:
  • iPhone: Settings → Safari → Notifications → Allow
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Chrome/Jr. Lancers → Notifications → On

Troubleshooting

"Add to Home Screen" not showing?

  • iPhone: Make sure you're using Safari, not Chrome
  • Android: Make sure you're using Chrome, not another browser
  • Try refreshing the page
  • Make sure you're on the correct URL: lancers-bball.web.app

Can't register?

  • Make sure you're using the email address you provided to Coach Logan
  • Only pre-approved parent/coach emails can register
  • If you're a viewer (grandparent, etc.), ask your family member to invite you first
  • Contact Coach Logan if you're still having issues

Verification email not arriving?

  • Check your spam/junk folder
  • Wait a few minutes - sometimes there's a delay
  • Try registering again with the same email
  • Make sure you entered your email correctly

App not loading or showing errors?

  • Check your internet connection
  • Try refreshing or closing and reopening the app
  • Clear your browser cache (Settings → Safari/Chrome → Clear History)
  • Make sure you're using a supported browser (Safari on iOS, Chrome on Android)

Notifications not working?

  • Check that notifications are enabled in your phone's settings
  • On iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Safari/Jr. Lancers → Allow Notifications
  • On Android: Settings → Apps → Chrome/Jr. Lancers → Notifications → On
  • Make sure you tapped "Allow" when first prompted
