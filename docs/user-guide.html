Inviting Viewers (Family Members)

Parents can invite grandparents, aunts, uncles, or other family to follow the team.

To invite a viewer:

  1. Open your child's player profile
  2. Scroll to "Invited Viewers"
  3. Tap "+ Add"
  4. Enter their email, name, and relationship
  5. Tap "Send Invite"

They'll receive an email with registration instructions.

Note: Viewers have limited access - they can see schedules, stats, and highlights, but cannot access team chat or the playbook.

Playbook

View all the team's plays with animated diagrams. Great for practicing at home!

Playbook with plays

Play animation

How to Use:

  1. Select a category: VS MAN, VS ZONE, or INBOUND
  2. Choose a play from the list
  3. Use the "Highlight" dropdown to show your child's position highlighted on the court
  4. Tap "Go" to watch the play animate step-by-step
  5. Read the steps below the diagram

Select your child's position (1-5) from the "Highlight" dropdown to see their movements emphasized during animation. This makes it easy to understand exactly what your player does in each play.

Practice at home! Walk through plays with your child using the animated diagrams. Knowing the plays builds confidence on the court.

Play Designer with court and action tools

Recording Missed Shots

Long-press (hold) the +2, +3, or +1 button to record a missed shot:

  1. Press and hold the button
  2. A "MISS?" indicator appears at the top
  3. Keep holding until it changes to "MISS!"
  4. The missed shot is recorded with a red flash

Tracking missed shots enables accurate shooting percentage calculations.

Opponent Score

Use the OPP row at the bottom to track the opponent's score:

  • +2, +3, +1 - Add points
  • -1 - Subtract a point (to correct mistakes)

Substitutions

To make substitutions:

  1. Tap the "SUB" button
  2. Players on court are shown at the top
  3. Players on bench are shown at the bottom
  4. Tap a player on court to select them for removal
  5. Tap a player on bench to put them in
  6. The swap happens automatically
  7. Tap X to close when done

Always keep exactly 5 players on court.

Quarter Changes

Tap the quarter buttons to advance the game:

  • Q1 → Q2 → HALF → Q3 → Q4 → END GAME

The current quarter is shown in the header.

Timeouts

Use the timeout buttons to track timeouts:

  • TIMEOUT - Lancers timeout (shows remaining count)
  • OPP TIMEOUT - Opponent timeout

Youth rules: 4 timeouts per half, 2 per overtime.

Ending the Game

After Q4, tap "END GAME" to finalize stats. This locks the stats and triggers the game wrap-up process.

Coach: Editing Stats After the Game

Coaches can edit stats for completed games:

  1. Go to Stats tab

Upload form

Viewing Highlights

  • Browse all highlights in a grid
  • Filter by game - Select a specific game
  • Filter by player - See one player's highlights

Uploading Highlights

To upload a highlight:

  1. Tap the camera icon (upload button)
  2. Select your photo or video
  3. Choose which game it's from
  4. Select which player is featured
  5. Add a caption (optional)
  6. Tap "Upload"

Smart Timestamp Matching

Auto-matching: When you upload a photo or video taken during a game, the app reads the file's timestamp and tries to match it to a specific play in the play-by-play feed.

If a match is found, the game and player are automatically selected, and the highlight gets linked to that specific play!

Highlights appear in multiple places:

  • Main Highlights gallery
  • Game detail page
  • Player profile modal
  • Play-by-play feed (linked plays show a camera icon)

Game Wrap-Ups

After each game, a wrap-up summary is generated with highlights of the game.

Game wrap-up section

What's in a Wrap-Up:

  • Narrative Summary - AI-generated game recap describing key moments
  • Coach's Notes - Commentary from Coach about the game
  • Player of the Game - Standout performer with their stats
  • Highlight Reel - Compiled video of game highlights (when available)

How It Works:

  1. Game ends (scorekeeper taps "END GAME")
  2. Coach has a window to add notes and shoutouts
  3. AI generates a narrative summary based on stats
  4. Coach reviews and approves
  5. Everyone receives a notification when published

Find wrap-ups at the bottom of any completed game's detail page.

For Viewers (Grandparents, Relatives)

Viewers are family members invited by parents to follow the team.

Viewer home screen

Limited navigation

Viewers CAN:

  • View the schedule and game results
  • View the roster and player stats
  • View and upload highlights
  • View game details, box scores, and wrap-ups
  • View live stats during games

Viewers CANNOT:

  • Access team chat or posts (private team communication)
  • View the playbook (team strategy)
  • RSVP for attendance
  • Sign up to volunteer

How to Get Invited

Tips & Quick Reference

Game Day Checklist

  1. RSVP - Make sure you've responded for the game
  2. Check Chat - Look for any last-minute updates
  3. Review Plays - Practice with your child using the Playbook
  4. Arrive Early - Be there for warm-ups
  5. Take Photos/Videos - Upload them as highlights!
  6. If Scorekeeping - Arrive 10 min early to get set up
